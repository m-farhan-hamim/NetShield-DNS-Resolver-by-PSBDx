Starting from the app, widgets or the Quick Settings tile now verifies the connection to your DNS upstream first; on failure you get the exact error and can switch to Google UDP.
Fixed DNS-over-HTTPS and DNS-over-TLS timing out while the VPN is active: the upstream is now reached over the real network instead of looping through NetShield itself.
Fixed corrupt launcher icon images (replaced with valid PNGs).
The app now checks for a working internet connection: when offline, Start is disabled and a banner explains why.
